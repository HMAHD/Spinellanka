# Surface brief — Public home

## Scope and mode

- Mode: Persuade
- Audience: international travelers planning transport in Sri Lanka before arrival
- Job: understand the four supported services, see how advance requests work, and choose the right service
- Primary action: choose one service and open its factual service page
- Proof: the four approved service definitions, exact-vehicle principle, and pending-until-confirmed workflow
- Constraints: no invented fleet facts, prices, support details, policies, reviews, claims, or imagery; WCAG 2.2 AA; responsive Blade-first delivery

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The page behaves like a calm mobility desk: a fixed service-selection object beside a concise reading column. It refuses glossy travel imagery and equal feature-card grids.

**OWN-WORLD:** Cloud canvas and Spinel Navy divide the page into clean fields; Ceylon Amber marks only the active service and primary action. Inter, square-edged paper slips, itinerary rows, generous spacing, and 10–12px functional corners carry the system without gradients, shadows, or invented pictures.

**STORY:** A traveler identifies the offer, chooses one of four services, learns that exact vehicles are selected and every request awaits staff confirmation, then reads the request sequence. The selected service opens a dedicated factual page.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** On desktop, a navy service desk occupies the left half and stays visible while the cloud reading column scrolls. The desk holds the wordmark, four-service selector, and amber “View service” action. The right half carries the two-line title, one concrete sentence, and the approved request conditions. On mobile, navigation leads, the title appears first, then the full-width service desk. The signature interaction is the live service selector; the selected option updates the desk summary without hiding the no-script list.

**FORM:** Split-stage mobility desk, first structural choice; `seed: none (pinned)` — “Use approved direction.”

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Unresolved inputs

Verified fleet records and photographs, prices, service-area boundaries, support contacts, policies, legal wording, provider choices, and production booking forms remain client-supplied.
