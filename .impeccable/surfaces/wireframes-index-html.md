# Surface brief — UX planning walkthrough

## Scope and mode

- Primary target: `wireframes/index.html`
- Mode: Read, with embedded Operate-mode screen studies
- Audience: client stakeholders approving product scope and UX; future designers and developers using the artifact as a reference
- Job: understand the four services, inspect complete customer and operations flows, identify missing client inputs, and record approval without mistaking low-fidelity structure for final UI
- Constraints: grayscale only; no invented brand, prices, policies, claims, vehicle details, or imagery; static review artifact rather than a production web app

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A numbered transport-operations storyboard makes every decision inspectable. It refuses the glossy travel landing page and the generic component-gallery arrangement because either would make placeholders look approved.

**OWN-WORLD:** Paper-white canvas, graphite text, hard black rules, one mid-gray status tone, square corners, mono identifiers, compact annotation tags, route lines, calendars, and deliberately skeletal image fields. Hierarchy comes from scale, rules, spacing, and numbering—not color, gradients, shadows, floating pills, or decorative imagery.

**STORY:** The reviewer first sees what is in and out, then follows one shared booking spine through four service variations, then inspects guest management, admin scheduling, rental handover, driver tasks, and failure states. Every frame states purpose, key decisions, and unresolved client content. The final section converts review into a sign-off checklist.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A narrow persistent document rail sits left. The main plane opens with the working title, LOW-FIDELITY / NOT FINAL UI marker, concise review instructions, four service definitions in a ruled comparison grid, and a single horizontal booking spine directly below. The primary action is “Start review,” leading to the first service frame—not a booking CTA that could be mistaken for a working app.

**FORM:** User-pinned neutral grayscale review document; first and only structural choice because high-fidelity identity was explicitly deferred. Seed key: `user-pinned-neutral-wireframe-no-roll`.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

At a glance, the fleet timeline frame aligns exact vehicles, permanent drivers, temporary checkout holds, pending requests, confirmed bookings, maintenance, and rental handovers on one ruled schedule—making the product's availability mechanism visible without visual polish.

## Unresolved inputs

Actual product/brand name, all five vehicle records and photos, pricing values, exchange-rate policy, eligibility thresholds, cancellation and wait cutoffs, tax details, support contacts, legal wording, verified proof/reviews, gateway/provider choices, and final content remain client-supplied.
