# 07 — Client Content and Business-Input Checklist

**Purpose:** collect the real information required to replace wireframe placeholders and make later design/development safe.

## How to use this document

For every item, record:

- **Owner:** person responsible for supplying it.
- **Source:** document/system/person proving the value.
- **Status:** Not started / Draft / Verified / Approved.
- **Approved by/date:** person authorized to bind the business.
- **Notes/version:** conditions and effective date.

Do not treat a value copied from a competitor or reference website as client approval.

## 17. Integration accounts — later technical planning

Do not choose providers in this UX phase. Before implementation, identify and approve:

- map/place/route provider account, coverage, cost, privacy, and usage limits;
- Sri Lanka-compatible hosted payment provider, methods, currencies, refunds, and webhook support;
- WhatsApp Business account/provider/template approval process;
- transactional email domain/provider and authentication;
- exchange-rate source or audited manual process;
- hosting, monitoring, security, and backup choices.

## 18. Client decision meeting checklist

Before signing off UX, the client should bring:

- a person authorized to approve service definitions and operations;
- fleet/driver data owner;
- person responsible for rates/payments;
- person responsible for Rent/legal/insurance terms;
- brand/content owner;
- sample existing booking, quote, receipt/invoice, rental agreement, and handover forms if they exist.

## 19. Readiness gate

### UX may be approved with placeholders when

- the structure and behavior are approved;
- each placeholder has an identified client owner;
- missing values do not hide an unresolved product decision.

### High-fidelity UI should not finish until

- brand assets, real vehicle imagery, approved content hierarchy, and proof strategy are available.

### Development should not finalize until

- fleet facts, rates, availability rules, policy values, legal requirements, payment behavior, and operational responsibilities are verified and approved.
