Plan the journey before arrival.

This walkthrough validates structure, information, states, and operations for a no-login transport booking website. It deliberately contains no final brand, invented vehicle data, real prices, legal claims, or visual styling direction.

05

Recovery states

A simple product is not a happy-path-only product. Each state says what happened, what remains valid, and what the traveler can do next.

WF-14 · Reusable state boardNo dead ends
NO AVAILABILITY

No exact vehicle fits this window.

Show nearby times/dates and other suitable exact vehicles. Do not reveal another customer’s booking.

Try suggested timeAsk staff
CUSTOM QUOTE

We cannot calculate this route reliably.

Keep all entered details, remove the misleading estimate, and explain when staff will respond.

Request custom quote
HOLD EXPIRED

Your checkout hold ended.

Safe form progress remains. Recheck the same vehicle; if unavailable, show valid alternatives.

Recheck availability
STALE RESULT

Details changed after the last check.

Name the changed information and require a fresh price and availability check before submission.

Update result
PAYMENT PENDING

The provider has not confirmed payment yet.

Do not charge again automatically. Recheck status, show the booking impact, and provide support.

Check payment status
MESSAGE FAILED

Your request is still recorded.

Show the reference and private link, retry the failed channel, and alert staff where needed.

ResendCopy reference
LINK EXPIRED

This secure link is no longer valid.

Reveal no booking data. Offer a safe recovery route using the reference and verified contact.

Recover access
VEHICLE DISRUPTION

Your selected vehicle needs replacing.

Show the equal/better alternative and price effect. The traveler accepts it or chooses cancellation/refund.

Review replacement
A · State language Never use color alone. The status code, plain-language heading, explanation, and recovery action carry meaning together.
